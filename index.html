Welcome to The Brownie Bite

Our Purpose: To celebrate, educate, and share the joy of the ultimate baked good. Whether you love them fudgy, cakey, or loaded with nuts, this site honors the rich history and magic of the chocolate brownie.

The Origin Story

The brownie wasn't an accident; it was a crafted masterpiece. In 1893, socialite Bertha Palmer asked the pastry chef at the Palmer House Hotel in Chicago to create a dessert suitable for women attending the Chicago World's Columbian Exposition.


She requested a cake-like confection that could be easily eaten from boxed lunches. The result? The Palmer House Brownie, made with a decadent amount of chocolate, walnuts, and an apricot glaze. The hotel still serves the original recipe today!

Expert Research: Fun Facts & Statistics

  • The 3,000-Pound Behemoth: In 2001, a bakery in New York created the largest brownie on record. It weighed 3,000 pounds!
  • The Name's Origin: The word "brownie" originally referenced mythical elf-like creatures found in Scottish folklore. Early "brownie" recipes were actually molasses cakes.
  • Consumption Statistics: It is estimated that Americans consume over 1.5 billion brownies every year. Furthermore, 52% of people prefer "fudgy" brownies, while 24% prefer "cakey".
