The Brownie Bite

Purpose: Educating the world on the history and science of the perfect brownie.

History

Created in 1893 at the Palmer House Hotel for the World's Fair. Bertha Palmer wanted a "lady's dessert" that fit in a lunchbox.

Expert Research

  • Stat: 1.5 Billion brownies are eaten in the US annually.
  • Fact: The first "brownies" contained no chocolate (they were molasses based).
  • Fact: 52% of brownie fans prefer the corner/edge pieces.
