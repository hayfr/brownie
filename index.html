Brownie History

One of the earliest brownies was created at the Palmer House Hotel in Chicago for the 1893 World's Fair.

Bertha Palmer wanted a dessert that was smaller than cake and easy to include in boxed lunches.

Brownie Facts

  • Brownies can be fudgy, chewy, or cakey.
  • Walnuts and chocolate chips are common additions.
  • Brownies are usually baked in a square pan.
  • The edges normally cook faster than the center.
