The Ultimate Fudgy Brownie

A perfectly crinkly top with a dense, chocolatey center.

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount Notes
Unsalted Butter1/2 cup (115g)Melted & hot
Vegetable Oil1 tbspFor chewiness
White Sugar1 1/8 cup (225g)Creates the crinkle top
Large Eggs2Room temperature
Vanilla Extract2 tspEnhances flavor
All-Purpose Flour1/2 cup (65g)Sifted
Cocoa Powder1/2 cup (50g)Unsweetened

Instructions

  1. Preheat: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line an 8x8 pan with parchment.
  2. Mix Wet: Whisk hot melted butter, oil, and sugar for a minute. Add eggs and vanilla, beating until lighter in color.
  3. Add Dry: Sift in flour, cocoa powder, and salt. Fold gently with a spatula until just combined.
  4. Bake: Pour batter into pan. Bake 20-25 minutes. A toothpick in the center should come out dirty.
  5. Cool: Let cool completely before slicing. Enjoy!
