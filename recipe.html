The Ultimate Fudgy Brownie

A perfectly crinkly top with a dense, chocolatey center.

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount Notes
Unsalted Butter 1/2 cup (115g) Melted & hot
Vegetable Oil 1 tbsp For chewiness
White Sugar 1 1/8 cup (225g) Creates the crinkle top
Large Eggs 2 Room temperature
Vanilla Extract 2 tsp Enhances flavor
All-Purpose Flour 1/2 cup (65g) Sifted
Cocoa Powder 1/2 cup (50g) Unsweetened

Instructions

  1. Preheat: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Line an 8x8 inch pan with parchment paper.
  2. Mix Wet Ingredients: In a large bowl, whisk together the hot melted butter, oil, and sugar for about a minute. Add the eggs and vanilla, beating until lighter in color (this helps form the glossy top).
  3. Add Dry Ingredients: Sift in the flour, cocoa powder, and a pinch of salt. Gently fold the mixture with a spatula until *just* combined. Do not overmix!
  4. Bake: Pour the batter into the prepared pan. Bake for 20-25 minutes. A toothpick inserted in the center should come out dirty for fudgy brownies.
  5. Cool: Let them cool completely in the pan before slicing. Enjoy!
