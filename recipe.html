Brownie Recipe

Relatively easy brownie recipe

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Butter 1/2 cup
Sugar 1 cup
Eggs 2
Vanilla 1 teaspoon
Cocoa powder 1/3 cup
Flour 1/2 cup
Salt 1/4 teaspoon

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Melt the butter and mix it with the sugar.
  3. Add the eggs and vanilla, then stir.
  4. Mix in the cocoa powder, flour, and salt.
  5. Pour the batter into a greased 8-inch square pan.
  6. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes.
  7. Let the brownies cool before cutting them.
